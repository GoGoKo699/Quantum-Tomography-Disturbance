# Scientific reading route

The question is the cumulative measurement disturbance required to learn an unknown qubit to a specified final accuracy. The source radius is known, its direction is unknown, and each specimen undergoes one adaptive projective measurement before release.

1. [Model and physical cost](model.md): chronology, Lüders instruments, fidelity conventions and minimax quantifiers.
2. [The joint frontier](frontier.md): the uniform law in sample count, mixedness and terminal accuracy.
3. [Adaptive learner](algorithm.md): the centered tangent-probe construction.
4. [Upper bound](upper_bound.md): exact moments, global expected-risk recursion and precision tuning.
5. [Lower bounds](lower_bounds.md): information per disturbance, biased-estimator converses and the pure axis-prediction cost.
6. [Resources and digital implementation](resources.md): preparations, controls, computation, precision and output.
7. [Interpretation](interpretation.md): why the two learning goals diverge away from purity and where the instrument restriction matters.

[Related work](../references/related_work.md) credits the pure endpoint, statistical tools and earlier information–disturbance comparisons. [Finite verification](../verification/README.md) independently checks selected identities and mechanisms; it does not replace the analytical proof.
